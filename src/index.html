Flagship Product
NervaGuard

Enterprise-grade secure document sharing and access-control platform. NervaGuard protects sensitive information across its entire lifecycle with dynamic forensic watermarking, instant revocation, and strict zero-trust viewing.

  • Dynamic forensic watermarking (viewer IP, email, timestamp)
  • Instant access revocation and session termination
  • Granular permissions, expiry dates & view-only DRM controls
  • Detailed audit trails & SIEM-ready access telemetry
Built for: Financial services, legal counsel, M&A diligence, and enterprise governance.
